# Talaran Rules (first draft)

*Draft for Nathan to review, 2026-11-07. Written plainly, as players will read it. Once agreed it becomes a page players can find from the home page, the Manual and sign-up.*

---

Talaran is a game for everyone, including younger players. These rules keep it fair and keep it a good place to spend time. Staff use their judgement in applying them, and may warn, mute or ban an account depending on what happened.

## 1. Your account

- **One account per person.** Do not make or use a second account for any reason, including trading with yourself, voting twice in polls, or holding more items or gold.
- **Your account is yours alone.** Do not share it, lend it, or play on someone else's.
- **Accounts are not for sale.** Do not buy, sell or trade accounts.
- **Keep your password private.** Staff will never ask for it. Anyone who does is not staff.
- You are responsible for everything done on your account.

## 2. Playing fairly

- **No bots, macros, scripts or auto-clickers.** You must be the one playing. Programs that click, answer bot checks or play for you are not allowed.
- **Answer bot checks yourself.**
- **No real-money trading.** Do not buy or sell gold, items, accounts or in-game services for real money or anything outside the game.
- **Report bugs; do not use them.** If you find something that gives you items, gold or experience it should not, stop and report it on the forum or through Support. Using a bug on purpose, or telling others how to, is against the rules.
- **Do not interfere with the game or its servers**, or try to get into anything that is not yours.

## 3. Names and pictures

- **Names must be suitable for everyone.** This covers your character name, guild names and tags, and shop names. Nothing rude, sexual, hateful or offensive, including words hidden with spelling tricks.
- **Profile pictures follow the same rule.** Nothing sexual, violent, hateful or shocking.
- **Do not pretend to be staff**, or say you are staff when you are not. Do not pretend to be another player.
- Staff may change a name or remove a picture that breaks these rules.

## 4. Public spaces

Public spaces are world, region, trade and help chat, the forum, and anything other players can read, such as guild descriptions and shop descriptions. They are kept strict.

- **No swearing.** The game changes swear words automatically, but trying to get around it is still breaking the rule.
- **No sexual talk or content.**
- **No hate.** No slurs, and no attacks on anyone for their race, religion, nationality, gender, sexuality, disability or anything else about who they are.
- **No bullying, harassment or threats.** Do not target, follow or pick on another player.
- **Keep personal details private.** Do not share your own or anyone else's real name, address, phone number, school, photos or social media accounts.
- **No spam.** Do not flood chat, repeat the same message over and over, or post in capitals to shout.
- **No advertising** other games, websites or services.
- **No scams.** Do not lie to get items or gold, pretend to give things away, or trick anyone in a trade.
- **Use channels for what they are for.** Help chat is for questions, trade chat is for trading.
- **Keep heated real-world topics out**, such as politics. Talaran is a place to get away from them.

## 5. Private spaces

Guild chat, whispers and private messages are not filtered for swearing. The other rules still apply there: no harassment, hate, threats, sexual messages to other players, sharing personal details or scams.

## 6. Staff

- Staff are here to help and to keep the game fair. Please follow their instructions.
- If you disagree with a decision, ask about it calmly through Support. Do not argue it out in public chat.
- **Staff will never ask for your password.**

## 7. Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, tell a member of staff through Support or a private message, and include what happened and when. Do not take matters into your own hands.

## 8. Changes

These rules may change as the game grows. The newest version is always the one that applies.
