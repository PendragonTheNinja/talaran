# Talaran: Spoils of the Fight

> Fighting on Taiar now pays its way. Every creature leaves something of its own behind, and there are new things to make from what you bring home: a ratskin armour set for anyone starting out, and a ring and a charm for fighters.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Drops

- [Balance] **Every creature on Taiar now has a common drop of its own**, something with a use that is not food. Most of them leave it about one kill in four. Your Bestiary tells you who drops what as you go.
- [Content] **Four new materials:** **Ratskin**, **Jackalope Pelt**, **Crabshell Plate**, and **Shellback Plate**.
- [Balance] **Seeds, fuel, ore, and chests drop more often** from the creatures that carry them, and there are a few new seeds among them.
- [Balance] **Trophies used in crafting** (the Wolf Fang, the Agropelter Knuckle, and the Jackalope Antler) **drop more often** than before.
- [Balance] **Dock Rats no longer drop Bones.** Bones stay with Hunting.

## 🛡️ Armour

- [Content] **The Ratskin set**: Cap, Jerkin, Leggings, Gloves, and Boots, worn from **Defense 1**. Each is sewn from Ratskin alone, from **Crafting 1**. It is light armour for those with none: a little protection, and it does not last long.
- [Balance] **Crabshell armour is now sewn from plates** instead of dropping whole. Gauntlets, Sabatons, and Helm take **Crabshell Plates**; Shield, Greaves, and Cuirass take **Shellback Plates**. Each piece's armour and durability are unchanged. Any Crabshell you already own is untouched.
- [Balance] **The Wolfskin Cloak now takes a Wolf Fang** as well as its 20 Wolf Pelts.

## 💍 Fighter's Jewelry

- [Content] **Knuckle Ring**: **+3 power**, worn from **Melee 8**. Set at the lapidary from an Agropelter Knuckle, an Ambren Ingot, and 10 Ratskin, from **Crafting 8**.
- [Content] **Jackalope Charm**: **+12 aim**, worn from **Melee 10**. Made at the lapidary from a Jackalope Antler and 10 Jackalope Pelts, from **Crafting 10**.
- [Feature] **Power and aim now count from any worn slot**, not just the weapon, so rings and charms help in a fight.
