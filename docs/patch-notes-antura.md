# Talaran 1.25: Antura

> A second island lies across the water from Talador. Antura is tier 2: new ore, new metal, new crops, new fish, new dishes, new creatures, and new things to make from all of them, for players from level 13. Getting there is a ferry ride, or a boat of your own, and the sea between has islets on it worth landing on.

# The Short Version

*No spoilers here. The full notes follow below for anyone who wants every number.*

## A New Island

- [Content] **Antura, Talaran's second island**, with eight places to find, each with its own work, people, and trouble. Most of it opens at **level 13**.
- [Feature] **A ferry crosses from Talador to Wendhaven** for a fare, and anyone may take it.
- [Content] **A new metal, a new tier of tools, arms, and armor**, and the ore to make them from.
- [Content] **New crops, new forage, new fish, and a new page of dishes**, including two new provisions.
- [Feature] **Ale**, brewed slowly and best shared. A round stood at a tavern helps everyone there.
- [Content] **New creatures to fight**, with new armor, a cloak, a ring, a charm, and three rare weapons to find among them.
- [Content] **New garnet jewelry** for Talar.
- [Content] **Antura has its own museum, its own merchants, its own tavern, and its own quests.**

## Boats and the Sea

- [Feature] **Build your own boat** on a slip, a piece at a time, alone or with hired help. Your own boat crosses faster than the ferry.
- [Feature] **Put to sea and explore each island's islets.** Every landing turns up something, sometimes a great deal, sometimes something that does not want to be found.
- [Feature] **A ship's log** keeps every voyage, watch by watch.
- [Feature] **Name an islet.** The first to land on one that has no name yet may put one forward.

## Building and Homes

- [Feature] **Tier 2 homesteads and shops**, built outright or raised from tier 1.
- [Feature] **Hire a carpenter** to build your homestead, your shop, your boat, or your guild's hall for you.
- [Feature] **Share your homestead's store** with one other player you trust.
- [Feature] **Every island has its own homestead ground and its own shop**, and its own tally board.

## Around Taiar

- [Content] **The Friddik brothers** have errands for anyone with quick feet.
- [Feature] **Tutors give a daily task** once you have finished their lesson, paid in experience, gold, and a tool of their trade.
- [Balance] **Gathering tools are on a merchant's shelf every day**, so nobody is ever shut out of a gathering trade.

## Everything Else

- [Feature] **Feats now pop up when earned**, and you can pin one to your screen to watch it fill.
- [Feature] **Swap weapons and shields mid-fight** from a new column in the fight view.
- [Balance] **Rare weapons last more than twice as long** before they can break.
- [Feature] **Whisper and Message buttons** on a player's profile, and a guild's member list from its tag.
- [UI] **Place pictures behind the scene**, as the art arrives, and a slimmer progress ring on skill icons.
- [Feature] **The chat box takes the color of the channel** you are about to send to.
- [Content] **The Manual explains gear and food properly**, and every bench page lists what goes on the bench.
- [Fix] **A long list of fixes**, from forum reports.

# The Full Notes

*Everything, with the numbers. Where a creature's drops and weaknesses are concerned, the Bestiary still tells those as you fight.*

## Getting to Antura

- [Content] **Antura** is tier 2, for levels 13 to 24. Its eight places are **Wendhaven** (the port), **Farefield** (farmland), **Mettlefell** (the forge town), **Underway** (the mine), **Bidewater** (a lake), **Farvick Narrows** (a strait and its tidal shore), **Yondereach** (the high passes), and **Vergehorns** (twin headlands).
- [Feature] **The ferry** runs between **Talador and Wendhaven**: **50 gold** a crossing, **8 minutes**, open to anyone, and it pays no Wayfaring.
- [Feature] **Your own boat makes the same crossing**, faster than the ferry, and pays Wayfaring at sea's rate. At a port, the slip, the ferry, and your own boats are all under one **Sail** entry in the place's links.
- [Content] **Antura's roads run a little longer** than Taiar's, as befits a bigger, wilder island.
- [UI] **The map heading names the island you are on**, and the minimap shows plain road buttons for a place whose picture is not drawn yet.

## Boats

- [Feature] **The Skiff**, a tier 1 rowing boat: **Carpentry 10** to build, sailed from **Wayfaring 1**. Its keel is laid on the slip at **Talador** and raised in four pieces: **Keel and ribs** (80 Lanai Planks, 160 Ambren Nails), **Planking** (140 Lanai Planks, 300 Ambren Nails), **Thwarts and oars** (30 Lanai Planks, 40 Ambren Nails), and **Lines** (30 Nettle Cordage).
- [Feature] **The Cog**, a tier 2 sailing boat: **Carpentry 19** to build, sailed from **Wayfaring 13**, on the slips at **Talador** and **Wendhaven**. Six pieces: **Keel and ribs** (150 Lanai Planks, 300 Serph Nails), **Planking** (300 Lanai Planks, 650 Serph Nails), **Deck** (110 Lanai Planks, 200 Serph Nails), **Mast and yard** (40 Lanai Planks, 50 Serph Nails), **Rigging** (60 Nettle Cordage), and **Sail** (20 Linen Cloth). The crossing takes about **5 minutes** in a Cog at Wayfaring 13.
- [Feature] **Building a boat** sets **1 to 5 materials** a turn into the hull, more often the higher your Carpentry, and pays Carpentry each turn. A built boat waits at whichever port you stand in.
- [Content] **Nettle Cordage**: twisted from **2 Stinging Nettle** at **Crafting 5**, in 30 seconds. It is the rope for every boat.
- [Feature] **An island's islets want a boat of that island's tier**: a Skiff for Taiar's, a Cog for Antura's.

## The Islets

- [Feature] **Put to sea** from Talador (or Wendhaven, in a Cog) and the voyage lands you on one of the island's eight islets, by chance. Some are reached often, some seldom, one rarely. **Sail on** to another islet up to **three landings** a voyage, then sail home.
- [Feature] **The strandline**: every landing has something washed up on the shore, a sure small find of coins, crop seeds, bait, and now and then a Bird Nest or a locked chest.
- [Feature] **The find**: every landing also rolls one find of the islet's own, yours alone, and gone when you sail off. A work find is always the best of its kind you have the level for, with its good finds made **three times as likely** as on the mainland:
  - **A stand of trees**: 40 chops.
  - **An exposed vein**: a private vein of 25 to 40 ore, dense ore and gems likelier.
  - **A shoal**: 40 casts into the island's best water, better and heavier fish, and no bait used.
  - **A wild patch**: 40 picks at the island's best habitat, notable finds likelier.
  - **A game trail**: 15 hunts at the island's best quarry, surer shots, trophies likelier.
  - Other landings turn up **young animals** (chicks, calves, piglets, and now and then a foal), **wild flowers**, **birds' nests**, **a buried chest**, **an artefact**, or **a guarded cache**.
- [Content] **Taiar's islets**: the Wooded Islet, the Rocky Islet, the Sandbar, the Reef, the Heath Islet, the Sea Stack, the Drowned Quarry, and the Ruin. **Antura's islets**: the Pine Holm, the Granite Skerry, the Shingle Spit, the Kelp Reef, the Grass Holm, the Bird Cliff, the Tide-Cut Quarry, and the Wreck.
- [Content] **Guardians**: a guarded cache is watched by something that came out of the sea. Taiar has the **Trow** (level 9), the **Siren** (level 10), and the **Draugr** (level 12); Antura has the **Blue Man of the Minch** (level 15), the **Each-uisge** (level 17), and the **Nuckelavee** (level 20). Each is one long fight, paid like several ordinary kills, and some of them do more than hit back. Bring food.
- [Content] **Chests**: a guardian always leaves a **Locked Sea Chest** (Taiar) or a **Locked Antura Chest** (Antura), and now and then a keepsake of its own. **Geoffrey in Emberra picks the locks** for a fee: **25 gold** for a Locked Rusty Chest, **100** for a Locked Sea Chest, **200** for a Locked Antura Chest. You open the chest yourself, a short action that pays **Fishing**. Inside are coins, worked goods, ore, gems, and sometimes more.
- [Content] **The sailor's set**: pieces of a sailor's kit come out of the guardians' chests, and none repeats until you have found them all. **Each worn piece takes 1% off a sea journey.** Taiar's four are the **Tarred Hood**, **Rope-Worker's Gloves**, **Sea Boots**, and **Sounding Lead**; Antura's three are the **Oilskin Smock**, **Canvas Slops**, and **Boat Cloak**. Every piece is worn from level 1. A piece is certain by your sixth chest without one. One worn piece wears a little each sea journey.
- [Content] **Artefacts**: the Ruin and the Wreck hold things left by whoever lived out there. Taiar's five are the **Carved Lintel Stone**, **Weathered Figurine**, **Cracked Clay Lamp**, **Ambren Door Key**, and **String of Sea-Glass Beads**; Antura's four are the **Ship's Bell Clapper**, **Figurehead's Hand**, **Navigator's Bowl**, and **Sealed Letter Case**. Each island's museum has a new case, **From the Islets**, for them and for the guardians' keepsakes.
- [Content] **Someone you may remember** has been seen out on the islets, once on each island. He insists he has not left the square. Meeting him starts a quest, **Keep an Eye Out for Quank**, finished on the next island's islets.
- [Feature] **The ship's log** writes every voyage watch by watch: weather set at sea, stories told over several watches, and, very rarely, a sighting that leaves a **Fathom Scale** in your hands.
- [Feature] **Islet names**: the first player to land on an islet with no name may put a name and a note to the **Harbor Council**, but only while they stand there. If the council turns it down, the next to land may try. Chosen names are announced to everyone. These are alpha names and will be cleared at 1.0.
- [Feature] **Nobody is stranded.** A sailor who leaves the game on an islet is brought home with everything they carry. Falling to a guardian leaves your things at the port the voyage left from.
- [Content] **Two new hidden feats**, for those who find what is out there.

## Mining and Smithing

- [Content] **Underway Granite**, from **Mining 13**: a 30-second swing, with its own gem odds. **Garnet** is Antura's common gem, Quartz still turns up, and **Rough Amethyst** is the rare one.
- [Content] **Serph Ore**, from **Mining 13** at Underway, and **Serph veins**, a little rarer for the time spent than Ambren's.
- [Content] **Serph smelting**: Serph smelts alone, with **2 Charc** a bar. Charc stays a Taiar fuel for now.
- [Content] **44 Serph recipes** from **Smithing 13 to 24**, the twin of every Ambren recipe: every tool, every weapon, every armor piece, nails, hooks, and **Serph Arrows**.
- [Balance] **The Ambren Butchering Knife pays 55 Smithing XP** to make, up from 35.

## Woodcutting, Carpentry, and Building

- [Balance] **Deep Lanai is now tier 2 work.** A Serph Hatchet cuts it at its stated pace; an Ambren Hatchet still can, slower.
- [Balance] **Sawing pays 31 XP** a saw from **Carpentry 13**, up from 23.
- [Feature] **Geossica's bench in Verdale lends its own saw**, so a new carpenter can saw and make saw-work before owning a saw. A saw now wears only at your own bench.
- [Feature] **Tier 2 homestead**: storage for **80** kinds of thing, at **Carpentry 13**. Built outright (750 Lanai Planks, 750 Granite Blocks, 1,500 Serph Nails, 15 minutes) or raised from tier 1 (250, 250, and 500, in 5 minutes).
- [Feature] **Tier 2 shop**: storage for **100**, **18** selling slots and **8** buying slots, at **Carpentry 13**. Built outright (450 Lanai Planks, 450 Granite Blocks, 900 Serph Nails, 10 minutes) or raised from tier 1 (100, 100, and 200, in 2 minutes).
- [Feature] **Hire a carpenter.** Stand at the place, name the carpenter, and lay the fee and any materials; they build your homestead or shop with their own hands, and the fee is theirs when it stands. Boats and guild halls hire by the piece: name hands, set a wage a piece, and lay a purse at the slip or the hall. Whatever is left over comes back.
- [Balance] **Guild halls take 1 to 3 pieces** a build turn, more often 3 the better the carpenter. A hall's **tier 2 wants Carpentry 22**.
- [Feature] **One homestead and one shop on every island.** Antura's homestead ground is **Farefield**, holding at most **10 fields**; its shop is raised in **Wendhaven**. Each island's shop is managed in its own port.
- [Balance] **Fields and pens count across every homestead you own**, highest-tier island first, so a new island's fields never start the share over. The farm screen says when another island's fields count ahead of these.
- [Feature] **Tally boards are per island**, each with its own licence and built from its island's nails.

## Farming, Foraging, and Husbandry

- [Content] **Six new crops**, grown only in Antura's soil: **Barley** (Farming 13), **Cabbage** (15), **Leek** (17), **Beet** (19), **Broad Bean** (21, restores the soil), and **Gooseberry** (23, a bush that regrows). Grow times run from **22 to 40 hours**.
- [Content] **Barley** is harvested as itself and milled into Flour, **3 to 2**.
- [Content] **The Seed Ground** at Farefield, from **Foraging 13**: the seeds of all six crops.
- [Content] **The Tidal Shore** at Farvick Narrows, from **Foraging 19**: **Samphire** and **Sea Salt**.
- [Feature] **Animals eat what they really would.** Each species has a list of feeds (grain, barley, peas, and beans at its usual amount; roots, greens, and nuts at twice), chosen by hand in the Animals tab. Feed All and the Larder Ward use the cheapest you hold.
- [Balance] **Turnip no longer drains the soil.**
- [Content] **New walking finds on Antura's roads**: Sea Thrift, Spring Gentian, Feathers on the shore roads, Meadowsweet, a Wayfarer's Star, a Bent Horseshoe, and, very rarely, a Lit Garnet. The Tarnished Coin, the Chipped Arrowhead, and the Writ of Sending now turn up on every island.

## Fishing

- [Content] **Bidewater**: **Tench** (Fishing 13), **Rudd** (16), **Zander** (19), and **Glassback** (22), which rises only after dark.
- [Content] **Farvick Narrows**: **Mackerel** (13), **Pollock** (16), **Sea Bass** (19), and **Turbot** (22).
- [Content] **The Serph Fishing Rod**, and casting and netting times that follow each water's tier.
- [Balance] **Fishing rods last about twice as long** before they break, and **the Fishing Net about six times**, in step with what they cost to make.

## Cooking and Brewing

- [Content] **Eight cooked fish**, from Cooking 13 to 22.
- [Content] **Thirteen new dishes**, from Cooking 19 to 24: Buttered Samphire, Barley Pottage, Buttered Worts, Salt Fish, Bean and Barley Stew, Pickled Beet, Mackerel in Gooseberry Sauce, Leek and Cheese Tart, Beans and Pork, Samphire and Fish Broth, Gooseberry Fool, Salt Beef and Cabbage, and **Shore Pie**, which mends **132**.
- [Content] **Two new provisions**: **Crafter's Pottage** (Cooking 20) makes **Crafting 1.5% faster for 5 hours**; **Cook's Supper** (Cooking 21) does the same for **Cooking**.
- [Feature] **A pinch of salt**: switch it on in the cooking menu and each dish spends one Sea Salt, for a **10% chance of a second dish**.
- [Feature] **Ale**, from **Cooking 20**, brewed in the cask at **Mettlefell**: each batch takes **3 Barley and 1 Meadowsweet** and gives **4 Ale** after **6 hours**, with **60 Cooking XP** a batch. A cask takes **10 batches at Cooking 20**, rising to **25 from Cooking 35**.
- [Feature] **Ale is for company.** Alone it does nothing. **Stand a round** at a tavern for at least **3 players** there, one Ale each, and everyone gets **every skill 1% faster for 6 hours**, in a slot of its own beside your provision.
- [Content] **The Gaster's Gaze**, Wendhaven's taproom, kept by **Odda Fenn**. She sells Ale at **30 gold**, dear on purpose.

## Hunting and Trapping

- [Feature] **A hunt shoots the best arrow you carry.** An arrow a tier below the animal lowers your chance to catch it; one a tier above raises it a little. The hunting panel says when your arrows are too weak.
- [Content] **Serph Arrows**, made at the smithy.
- [Balance] **A Wild Sow now gives 1 to 2 Pork** beside her Piglet, when you reach her in time.

## Combat

- [Content] **Eight creatures** at four places: **The Burners' Yard** at Mettlefell (the **Wolverine**, level 13), **The Haunted Shallows** at Farvick Narrows (the **Kelpie**, 15, and the **Shellycoat**, 17), **The Horns** at Vergehorns (the **Sea Eagle**, 19, the **Reaver**, 20, and the **Snallygaster**, 21), and **The High Passes** at Yondereach (the **Cave Bear**, 22, and the **Slide-rock Bolter**, 24).
- [Content] **The Snallyscale set**, sewn from Snallyscales: **Gauntlets** (Crafting 14, worn from Defense 19), **Sabatons** (15, 20), **Helm** (16, 21), **Shield** (18, 22), **Greaves** (19, 23), and **Cuirass** (21, 24).
- [Content] **The Bearverine Mantle**, a tier 2 cloak of Cave Bear and Wolverine pelts: **armor 7**, sewn at **Crafting 20**, worn from **Defense 20**.
- [Content] **The Heartstone Ring**: **+4 power**, set at **Crafting 18**, worn from **Melee 18**.
- [Content] **The Kelpie's Bridle Charm**: **+14 aim**, made at **Crafting 20**, worn from **Melee 20**.
- [Content] **Three rare weapons**: **Eagle Talons** (a pair, pierce, aim 185, power 61, Melee 19), the **Reaver's Dane Axe** (two-handed, slash, aim 175, power 103, Melee 20), and the **Dripstone Cudgel** (one-handed, crush, aim 165, power 60, Melee 22). The Cudgel drops 1 kill in **750**, the other two 1 in **1,000**, each certain by twice that.
- [Balance] **The Hodag Horn** is raised to **81 power**, to stand level with the Serph Atgeir.
- [Balance] **Rare weapons last far longer.** The Hodag Horn, Wrecker's Cutlass, and Gouger's Legbone now take **5,000** uses before they can break, up from 2,000, and after that break only **0.1%** of the time, down from 0.2%. Antura's three take **6,250** uses, then **0.1%**. What you have already used counts toward the new number.
- [Balance] **The Gouger's Legbone drops more often**, **1 in 750** kills instead of 1 in 1,000, and the **Wrecker's Cutlass 1 in 1,000** instead of 1 in 1,250. The point at which one is certain moves with them.
- [Feature] **Swap weapons mid-fight.** The weapons in your pack run down the right of the fight view, with your shields beneath them while you hold a one-handed weapon. Tap one to take it up; the fight is played up to that moment with what you held before.
- [Balance] **The Agropelter drops Lanai Bark**, 1 to 2, about every other kill.

## Talar

- [Content] **Garnet jewelry**: the six Talar pieces again in **Serph and garnet**, tier 2: the **Garnet Keeper's Band**, **Prospector's Ring**, and **Seedsaver's Ring** (set at **Crafting 20**), and the **Garnet Hearty Amulet**, **Wayfinder's Amulet**, and **Baitkeeper's Amulet** (**Crafting 22**), each from a lit, bright, or radiant garnet, worn from **Talar 13**.

## Quests, Errands, and Dailies

- [Content] **Antura's quests**: **Nan** in Wendhaven keeps a window box and has three things to ask (**Nan's Window Box**, **Something Nobody Else Has**, and **Over the Edge**); **Aldous Penhallow**, clerk to the harbormaster, has **A Complaint Regarding the Snallygaster**; and in Farefield, **Wat Greening** and **Ebba Furrow** both grow cabbages and both want your help (**Wat's Cabbages** and **Ebba's Cabbages**), though you can only take one side.
- [Content] **The Draugr's Bar Tab**: Odda Fenn at the Gaster's Gaze is owed money by a dead man, and he can be found.
- [Feature] **The Friddik brothers**, Nathan in Verdale, Matthew at Dawncrest, and Christopher at Talar Rift, send **timed errands** between each other: a letter, a parcel, and sometimes everyday goods you bring yourself. Each leg pays about **40 gold and 75 Wayfaring XP**, with a limit worked out from the roads. **A late leg ends the errand** and pays only for goods you brought, and you are told exactly how late you were. An open errand shows in the Quests menu, counting down.
- [Feature] **Tutors give daily tasks.** Once you have finished a tutor's lesson, they ask for something each day: twenty minutes of skill experience, the goods' full value in gold, and a tool of their trade (Ambren below level 13, Serph from 13; Georome gives a cut stone for the rift). Between tasks they have more to say, and now and then something unexpected.
- [Feature] **Quests have chains and rivals.** A giver may have several quests in turn, and some quests close others.

## Museums and the Chronicle

- [Content] **The Antura Museum** in Wendhaven, with its own exhibits. Every item belongs to one island's museum.
- [Content] **From the Islets**, a new exhibit in both museums.
- [Balance] **The Four-Leaf Clover is no longer asked for by the museum**; it is too rare to ask a collector for. Anyone who gave one has it back.
- [Fix] **The Freshwater Pearl** has a museum case and a Manual page.
- [Feature] **The same find, told once.** When several people find the same rare thing on the same day, Wenna names them all in one line.
- [Content] **New Chronicle lines** for meeting someone familiar on the islets, and for finds made by more than one.

## Merchants and Trade

- [Content] **Antura's merchants**: **Wystan**, smith of Mettlefell (every Serph tool, arm, and armor piece), and **Ashby**, carpenter of Mettlefell; **Maude**, craftswoman of Wendhaven, **Gil** the provisioner (who only buys), and **Osric** the pawnbroker, in Wendhaven.
- [Balance] **Gathering tools are on the shelf every day**, 1 to 3 a day each, on top of the usual eight lines: **Merrick** keeps the Ambren Pickaxe, Hatchet, Foraging Knife, Hoe, and Butchering Knife; **Alder** keeps the Ambren Fishing Rod, Lanai Hunting Bow, Feed Pail, and Mucking Fork; **Tessa** keeps the Snare.
- [Feature] **Shared homestead stores.** Give one other player a key to your homestead's store from its Storage tab. They can put in and take out while standing there, and nothing more; your fields, pens, and building stay yours. You both see a list of who moved what. Either of you can end it at any time, from anywhere.
- [UI] **A broken tool tells you who sells another.**
- [UI] **Markets are named for their place**: Taiar Marketplace, Mettlefell Market, and Wendhaven Market.

## Players and Guilds

- [Feature] **Whisper and Message buttons** on a player's profile. Whisper fills the chat box (and opens Chat on a phone); Message opens a new letter to them.
- [Feature] **Tap a guild's tag** on a profile to see its members.
- [Feature] **Look into your guild's storehouses from anywhere**, from the Hall tab. Putting in and taking out still happen at the hall.

## Feats

- [Feature] **A feat now pops up when you earn it**, with the title or badge it gave you.
- [Feature] **Pin a feat** from the Feats panel and a small window keeps its progress on screen as you work. Drag it by its name, and resize it from any edge.

## Chat

- [Feature] **The chat box takes the color of where it will send**: gold for World, purple for Trade, teal for a whisper, and so on, so a wrong channel shows before you press Enter.
- [Fix] **Pressing Enter twice quickly no longer sends a line twice.**
- [Feature] **Spelling is underlined in chat**, with fixes on right-click, wherever there is a mouse. It stays off on phones, where it would wake autocorrect.

## Interface

- [UI] **Place pictures** can sit behind the scene's words, darkened for reading. They arrive as the art does; the Sea Stack is the first. Turn them off in **Settings > Themes**.
- [UI] **Skill progress is a thin gold line** on the edge of each skill icon, the same on every screen. **Stonehold** shows skill icons in full color.
- [UI] **Talaran time on the island map**, so you know when Geomima's fires are lit.
- [UI] **The homestead opens on the tab you last used.**
- [UI] **The equipment picker**: Take off is gone (a left-click on the slot does that), and right-clicking another slot while the picker is open opens that slot at once.
- [Feature] **The bell can ring when a one-off job is done**, such as mucking every pen or spreading manure. Tick it in the bell's list.
- [Fix] **The bell's list fits on a phone**, and scrolls.
- [UI] **NPCs no longer show an emoji** beside their names.
- [UI] **A place's links sit closer together** and lose their arrows, and the **Tally Board** is always the last of them.
- [UI] **The level-up pop-up names homestead, shop, and hall tiers**, sawing, and brewing as they open.

## The Manual

- [Content] **Every bench page lists its slots**: what fits each one, whether it is needed, and whether it wears out, with where the public bench stands.
- [Fix] **Smithing, Carpentry, and Tanning** no longer say your tools are used up when you set up a bench; they stay in it until you take them out.
- [Fix] **Woodcutting** says hatchet, not axe.
- [Content] **Item pages** for everything new: islet relics, the sailor's set, chests and their locks, errands, dailies, quest items, brewing, and rounds.
