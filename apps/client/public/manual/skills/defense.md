Defense is how hard you are to hit and how much damage your armor stops. It rises from fighting, and its level decides which armor you can wear.

## Where and how to start

Defense rises from fighting. On Taiar, start at **The Docks** in Talador, as for Melee. On Antura (Defense 13 and up), see [Melee](/manual/skills/melee) for the fighting places.

Every kill pays experience to Defense and Constitution, split by how the creature's blows were met. **What your armor stopped goes to Defense; what got through goes to Constitution.** So good armor trains Defense, and fighting without armor trains Constitution.

**Early on, blows that miss you also count toward Defense:** half a share at Defense 1, falling to nothing by Defense 13. After that, the split is only what your armor stopped.

## How armor works

Armor does two things:

1. It makes you harder to hit. Your defense is your armor plus what your Defense level gives you, and a creature's aim must beat it.
2. It reduces every blow that does land by a flat amount.

A **shield** is the biggest single piece, and you can only use one with a one-handed weapon.

## Tools and gear

- **Equipped:** armor in every slot your level allows, and a shield in your off hand if your weapon allows one.
- **Carried:** nothing. Armor only works when worn.

## Connections

- **Smithing** makes metal armor from **Mining**'s ore: Ambren, then Serph from Smithing 13. **Wystan** in Mettlefell sells every Serph piece.
- **Crafting** sews the rest from creature drops: Ratskin and Crabshell on Taiar, **Snallyscale** on Antura (worn from Defense 19 to 24), and cloaks: the Wolfskin and the **Bearverine Mantle** (Defense 20). No armor drops whole; each island's best set is sewn from its own armored creature.
- **Melee**: your weapon decides whether you can use a shield.
- **Constitution** takes what your armor lets through.

Armor wears out and breaks. See [Combat](/manual/systems/combat).
