Defense is the art of being hit less, and of minding less when you are.

I came to it late. For my first season I believed that the way to win a fight was to end it quickly, and I have the scars to show how often that worked. The fighters who last are the ones who can stand still under a blow and be mildly annoyed by it.

## Where and how to start

There is nowhere to go to train Defense. It rises from fighting: on Taiar, start where Melee starts, at **The Docks** in Talador.

Every kill pays Defense and Constitution between them. How it is shared depends on how the creature's blows were met. **What your armour turned aside goes to Defense. What got through goes to Constitution.** A fighter in good armour therefore trains Defense, and a fighter in his shirt trains Constitution, whether he meant to or not.

## How armour works

Armour does two things, and both are worth having:

1. It makes you harder to hit at all. Your defence is your armour added to what your Defense level gives you, and a creature's aim must beat it.
2. It takes a bite out of every blow that does land, a flat amount however hard the blow.

A **shield** is the largest single piece, and you can only carry one beside a one-handed weapon. A fighter with a pair of blades or a great two-handed thing has made a choice, and the choice was to be hit more often.

## Tools and gear

**Equipped:** the armour itself, in every slot the level allows, and a shield in the off hand if your weapon leaves room.

**Carried:** nothing. Armour is the one kit you cannot leave in your pack and still have work.

## Connections

- **Smithing** forges the armour, one new piece for each smith's level through a metal, from **Mining**'s ore.
- **Crafting** makes the leather that lines it.
- **Melee** decides whether you may carry a shield at all.
- **Constitution** is the other half of the bargain: what Defense lets through, Constitution must survive.

Armour wears, like everything else you fight in. Read the ledger on the [Combat](/manual/systems/combat) page before you grow fond of a helmet.
