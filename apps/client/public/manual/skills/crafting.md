Crafting turns other trades' raw materials into finished goods: hides into buckskin and leather, rough gems into cut stones and jewelry, pelts and plates into armor, and plant fiber into rope, snares, and nets.

## What Crafting is for

Crafting usually comes at the end of a chain. The hunter brings hides, the miner brings gems, the farmer and forager bring flax and nettles, and Crafting makes them into goods. You need a supply of materials, from your own gathering or from other players.

## The disciplines

There are four, each with its own tab: **Tanning**, the **Lapidary**, **Sewing** (armor and cloaks from creature drops), and **Camp Work** (small items that need no bench). All of them raise the same Crafting level.

- **Taiar:** all of it is done in **Caliwen**.
- **Antura:** **Wendhaven** has room for your own lapidary, so garnets can be cut near where they are mined, and a **Crafting Bench** for sewing and camp work, so Antura's armor sets are made on Antura. Tanning, the spinning wheel, and the loom are in Caliwen only.

## Levels add capacity

In some disciplines, your level adds capacity as well as recipes. In Tanning, higher levels let you run more vats at once.

{{tab:Tanning}}

Tanning runs by itself: you load a vat, leave, and come back when it is done.

## Setting up a tannery

You need:

- A **Lanai Tanning Rack**
- A **Lanai Tanning Barrel**

Both are made with Carpentry and fitted where you mean to tan. They stay at that location and never wear out.

{{data:bench-tools:tanning}}

Hides come from hunting, so a tannery near the hunting grounds saves carrying.

## How a soak works

Load a vat with up to **ten hides** and leave. The soak runs on a fixed timer, online or not. When it finishes, it waits for you; nothing spoils if you collect late.

## How many vats

- One to start
- Two at Crafting 10
- Three at 20
- Four at 30
- Five at 40

Each vat soaks its own hide type on its own timer.

## Buckskin and leather

- **Buckskin** comes from hunted wild animals. The amount depends on the animal.
- **Leather** comes from cattle raised with Husbandry, and comes in tiers.

They are separate materials. A recipe that asks for one does not accept the other.

{{tab:Lapidary}}

The lapidary cuts rough gems and sets filled gems into jewelry.

## The bench

- A **Lanai Lap Wheel**, made with Carpentry.
- An **Ambren Gem Chisel**, made with Smithing, to cut.
- **Ambren Jeweller's Pliers**, made with Smithing, to set stones. Cutting does not need them.

On Taiar, **Geosper** keeps a public lapidary in Caliwen and lends all three to anyone who has done his lesson. It works at half speed. Your own works at full speed, and can be set up in **Caliwen** or in **Wendhaven** on Antura. Wendhaven has no public lapidary.

{{data:bench-tools:lapidary}}

Geosper also keeps a spinning wheel and a loom in Caliwen, open to everyone, for spinning thread and weaving cloth.

## Cutting

A rough gem comes off the wheel as one of three grades:

- **Cut**: most of the time.
- **Polished**: more often as your level rises above the stone's.
- **Flawless**: rarely, and less rarely at higher levels.

Grade decides how much light a stone holds once filled: Polished holds half again as much as Cut, and Flawless three times as much. See [Talar](/manual/skills/talar).

**Garnet** is Antura's gem, from Underway's granite, and is cut from **Crafting 13**. It needs a Serph chisel to cut at its normal pace, as quartz needs an Ambren one.

## Setting

A stone filled at the Rift can be set into a ring or amulet: quartz in Ambren, and from **Crafting 20**, garnet in **Serph**, for the tier 2 versions of the same six pieces. Each piece takes **three Lit, two Bright, or one Radiant** stones, and comes out the same either way. What each piece does is on the [Talar](/manual/skills/talar) page.

A few pieces use creature parts instead of light: the **Knuckle Ring** and **Jackalope Charm** on Taiar, and the **Heartstone Ring** and **Kelpie's Bridle Charm** on Antura. They add aim or power for fighting; see [Melee](/manual/skills/melee).

{{tab:Sewing}}

Armor is sewn from what creatures leave behind: pelts, plates, and scales. No armor piece drops whole from a creature; every set is made here.

## The sets

- **Ratskin** (Taiar): the beginner's set, from rats.
- **Crabshell** (Taiar): from the plates of the shore's armored creatures.
- **Snallyscale** (Antura): from **Snallyscales**. Gauntlets at **Crafting 14**, then Sabatons, Helm, Shield, and Greaves, up to the Cuirass at **21**. Worn from **Defense 19** to **24**, depending on the piece.
- Cloaks: the **Wolfskin Cloak** (Taiar), and the **Bearverine Mantle** (Antura), from Cave Bear and Wolverine pelts, at **Crafting 20**.

Crafting makes a piece; your Defense level decides whether you can wear it. The [Defense](/manual/skills/defense) page lists every piece.

{{tab:Camp Work}}

Small items that need no bench:

- **Leather Strips**, cut from leather, used in many armor pieces and tools.
- **Nettle Cordage**, from **Crafting 5**: two stinging nettles twisted into rope in thirty seconds. Boats need a lot of it.
- **Snares** for trapping, **Fishing Nets**, and the **Halter & Lead** for bringing in a horse.
- The **Straw Skep** for catching bees, the **Foraging Basket**, and **Leather Foraging Gloves**.
- **Granite Blocks**, dressed from raw granite, used by builders in the hundreds.

## Connections

- **Hunting** supplies hides.
- **Husbandry** supplies cattle, and so tiered leather.
- **Mining** finds the gems, and **Talar** fills them after cutting.
- **Carpentry** builds the racks, barrels, and benches, and uses your cordage for boats.
- **Combat** supplies the pelts, plates, and scales for armor.
- **Foraging** finds nettles and flax.
