Foraging searches wild ground for plants, berries, mushrooms, seeds, and odder finds. Each search gives one random find from that patch's list, so you do not choose what you get.

## Where and how to start

Go to a **habitat** (a patch of ground worth searching) and search it. Each search draws one find from that habitat's list. Common finds come often; rare ones seldom.

You do not see a habitat's list until you find things in it. Unfound entries show as question marks and fill in as you discover them.

**On Taiar**, the habitats are all at **Lanaivale**: the **Sunlit Meadow** from level 1, then the **Forest Floor**, the **Creekbank**, and the **Bramble Thicket** at higher levels.

**On Antura**:

- **The Seed Ground** at **Farefield**, from **Foraging 13**. It gives the seeds of every crop Antura grows. No merchant sells them, so this is where Antura's farming starts.
- **The Tidal Shore** at **Farvick Narrows**, from **Foraging 19**. It gives **Samphire** and **Sea Salt** for Cooking.

## Tools and gear

Three pieces, each in a different slot. Wear all three.

- **Foraging knife**, equipped in your main hand: each tier makes searching 5% faster, up to half the time.
- **Foraging gloves**, worn on your hands: some finds are thorny or prickly and never come up without gloves.
- **Foraging basket**, held in your off hand: gives one extra of whatever you find.

The Serph knife is the better blade from Foraging 13. **Merrick** in Talador sells Ambren knives.

## Connections

- **Cooking** uses herbs and edible finds. Antura's Sea Salt lets one dish make two (see [Cooking](/manual/skills/cooking)).
- **Farming** gets several of its seeds from foraging, and on Antura every seed comes from the Seed Ground.
- **Crafting** uses some of the less obvious finds.

## Other places to find things

**The road.** Walking between places, you sometimes find something at the roadside: mostly flowers, which bees use, and on Antura's roads sea thrift, spring gentian, meadowsweet for brewing, and feathers along the shore. Riding finds nothing. Road finds pay no Foraging experience, but you keep them.

**The islets.** A boat can land you at a wild patch: forty searches at the best habitat on that island you have the level for, with notable finds three times as likely as on the mainland. See [Sailing & the Islets](/manual/systems/sailing-and-the-islets).

## Why start it early

Foraging needs no bench, no property, and no level to begin. You can do it from your first hour.
