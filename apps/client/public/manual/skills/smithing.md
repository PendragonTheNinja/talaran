Smithing smelts ore into ingots at the forge and works ingots into tools, weapons, armor, nails, and arrowheads at the anvil. It also burns logs into Charc, the forge's fuel, in a kiln.

## Where and how to start

Smithing is done at a **smithy**: an anvil, a hammer, and tongs, each fitted into place where you work. Once fitted, they stay at that location; you can take any of them out again.

- **Emberra** (Taiar): **Geoffrey** keeps a public smithy. Once you have done his task, you can use it. It lends its own tools, wears none of them, and works at half speed. You can also set up your own smithy in Emberra.
- **Mettlefell** (Antura): you can set up your own smithy here, with a kiln. There is no public smithy on Antura. Mettlefell is a short walk from Underway's Serph.

## Ore and fuel

Smelting needs ore **and Charc**. Charc is used up with every smelt, so keep a supply.

## Metals

Each tier of smithing needs ore of that tier, so Smithing and Mining level together.

- **Ambren** (tier 1): one Ambren Ore, one Burgh Ore, and two Charc make two Ambren Ingots.
- **Serph** (tier 2, from **Smithing 13**): one Serph Ore and two Charc make one Serph Ingot. Antura has no kiln wood of its own, so Serph is smelted with Charc from Taiar.

From level 13 to 24, every Ambren recipe has a Serph version: tools, weapons, armor, nails, hooks, and arrowheads.

## Tool tiers and speed

The tools in your smithy have a tier, and so does the work.

- Work **of your tools' tier** goes at its normal pace.
- Work **one tier above** your tools takes about a third longer, and two tiers above longer again. Beyond that, the smithy refuses the work.
- Work **one tier below** your tools goes about an eighth faster.

An Ambren smithy can forge Serph slowly, so a Serph hammer is a good first Serph job.

{{tab:The Kiln}}

A kiln burns logs into **Charc** while you do something else.

## How it works

Load the kiln with logs, leave, and come back to collect the Charc. You can run **one burn at each place** you work.

Logs go in **twenty at a time**, as one batch.

**Log quality sets the yield:**

- Poor logs: **60 Charc** a batch
- Fine logs: **80 Charc** a batch
- Excellent logs: **100 Charc** a batch

**A burn takes three hours, however many batches you load**, so load it full.

## How many batches

Your Smithing level sets the batches per burn:

- One to start
- Two at Smithing 10
- Three at 20
- Four at 30
- Five at 40

At level 40 with excellent logs, one three-hour burn makes 500 Charc. Each batch also pays some Smithing experience.

Woodcutters get better logs as they level, so a smith who also cuts wood gets more Charc from each burn.

## Tools and gear

You do not hold anything to smith; the smithy holds the tools. You need the smithy, ore, and Charc.

If a tool breaks, Geoffrey's smithy lends its own, **Merrick** in Talador sells Ambren tools, and **Wystan** in Mettlefell sells every Serph tool, weapon, and piece of armor.

## Connections

- **Mining** supplies the ore.
- **Woodcutting** supplies logs for the kiln.
- **Hunting** needs arrowheads. A hunt shoots the best arrow you carry, so Serph arrows help against Antura's animals.
- **Carpentry** needs nails in large numbers: 1,000 for a farmstead.
- **Crafting**, **Farming**, and **Husbandry** need tools and fittings made here.
