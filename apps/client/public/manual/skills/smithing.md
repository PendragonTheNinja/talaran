Every trade in Talaran wants something from the smith, and the smith wants only ore and to be left alone. It is the most powerful negotiating position in the world and smiths know it.

## Where and how to start

Smithing begins with a **workstation**.

You need three things, and you must be carrying all of them:

- An **Anvil**
- A **Hammer**
- **Tongs**

Take them to a location and set up. The three are **consumed** in the setting up. They become the workstation; you do not get them back, and you do not carry them about afterwards.

This is not theft, whatever it feels like the first time. It is the difference between a tool and a *place*.

## What smithing actually does

Ore goes in. Metal comes out, and then goods come out of the metal.

The trade rises in tiers, and each tier wants ore of a matching grade, which means Smithing and Mining climb in lockstep. A smith who has outpaced his miner is a smith standing beside a cold forge with excellent technique.

## Connections

Smithing sits at the middle of nearly everything, which is why it is worth the setup cost.

- **Mining** feeds it entirely. These two are one trade wearing two hats.
- **Hunting** needs arrowheads. Every arrow in Talaran passes through a forge.
- **Carpentry** needs nails, and needs them by the thousand. A single farmstead wants a thousand of them.
- **Crafting**, **Farming**, and **Husbandry** all want tools and fittings that begin here.

If you have ever wondered why smiths seem to know everyone, it is not charm. It is that everyone eventually needs a nail.
