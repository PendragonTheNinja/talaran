Wayfaring is travel: on foot, on a mount, and by boat. Every journey pays it, and each level makes your journeys shorter.

## Where and how to start

Travel anywhere. Every journey over land pays Wayfaring, on foot or mounted, and each level shortens every journey after it. It needs no tool, bench, or teacher. [Travel & Mounts](/manual/systems/travel-and-mounts) has the full details of the roads.

## Ways to travel

- **On foot:** the most experience per journey, and the only way to find things at the roadside: flowers, feathers, the odd coin, and on Antura's roads some finds Taiar's do not have. Each level makes you faster.
- **Mounted:** faster from the start, and faster still as your level brings the mount toward its best pace. It pays a little under two thirds of what walking pays, and you find nothing on the road.
- **By your own boat:** the crossing to Antura and every voyage to the islets pay Wayfaring at about the rider's rate. A boat has a starting pace and a best pace, like a mount, and your level closes the gap.

**The ferry pays nothing.** It costs 50 gold and takes eight minutes from Talador to Wendhaven, open to anyone.

## What it opens

- **Mounts:** the **Novice's Pony** and the **Rouncey** from level 1, and the **Palfrey** from **Wayfaring 13**. Husbandry raises horses; Wayfaring decides who can ride them.
- **Boats:** the **Skiff** from **Wayfaring 1**, the **Cog** from **13**. Carpentry builds them; Wayfaring sails them. Each island's islets need a boat of that island's tier, so Antura's islets need a Cog.

## Tools and gear

**For walking:** a staff in your hands and good boots on your feet each take a little off every journey on foot. A **Wayfinder's Amulet**, filled with light, makes road finds more likely.

**For sailing:** the **sailor's set**, pieces of an old sailor's kit found in the islets' sea chests. **Each piece worn takes 1% off a sea journey.** All pieces can be worn from level 1. One worn piece wears a little each voyage.

**Mounts** go in their own slot. While riding, walking gear does nothing.

## Errands

The **Friddik brothers** send timed errands between each other: letters, parcels, and sometimes everyday goods you bring yourself. Each leg pays about 40 gold and 75 Wayfaring on top of the journey. Each leg has a time limit, based on the roads at a new walker's pace; a late leg ends the errand. See [Quests & People](/manual/systems/quests-and-npcs).

## Connections

- **Husbandry** raises the horses you ride.
- **Carpentry** builds the boats you sail.
- **Crafting** makes the cordage boats are rigged with.
- **Every other trade** needs you to get there.
