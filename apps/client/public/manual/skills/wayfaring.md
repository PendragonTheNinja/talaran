Wayfaring is the trade of getting there.

Everyone has it. Everyone trains it, whether they meant to or not, from the first time they walk out of Talador. It is the only trade in Talaran that you practise while doing something else entirely, and the only one I can promise you will never regret, because there is no road in this world that does not get shorter for it.

## Where and how to start

Walk somewhere. That is the whole of the beginning.

Every journey over land pays Wayfaring, on foot or mounted, and every level takes a little off every journey you make after it. You need no tool, no bench, and no teacher. The Travel & Mounts page has the full arithmetic of the roads; this page is the trade itself.

## Three ways to travel, one trade

- **On foot.** The most experience for the road, and the only way to find things at the verge: flowers, feathers, the odd coin, and on Antura's roads a few things you will not find on Taiar's. Every level makes you quicker.
- **Mounted.** Quicker from the first day, and quicker still as your Wayfaring closes the gap between a mount's starting pace and its best. It pays a little under two thirds of what walking pays, and you find nothing; you are too high up and going too fast.
- **At sea, in your own boat.** A crossing to Antura and every voyage out to the islets pay Wayfaring at about a rider's rate. A boat has a starting pace and a best pace, like a horse, and your Wayfaring closes that gap too.

**The ferry pays nothing.** It is fifty gold and eight minutes from Talador to Wendhaven, open to anyone, and you are a passenger. A passenger learns nothing about the sea except that it is wet.

## What it opens

- **Mounts.** The **Novice's Pony** and the **Rouncey** from your first day, and the **Palfrey**, smooth-gaited and bred for distance, from **Wayfaring 13**. Husbandry raises them; Wayfaring decides who may ride.
- **Boats.** The **Skiff** from **Wayfaring 1**, the **Cog** from **13**. Carpentry builds them; Wayfaring captains them. An island's islets want a boat of that island's tier, so Antura's islets wait for your Cog.

## Tools and gear

**Worn, for the road:** a staff in your hands and good boots on your feet each take a little off every journey on foot. A **Wayfinder's Amulet**, filled with light, makes a find on the road likelier.

**Worn, for the sea:** the **sailor's set**, the pieces of an old sailor's kit that turn up in the islets' sea chests. **Each piece worn takes one percent off a sea journey**, and all of them are worn from level 1. One worn piece wears a little each voyage.

**Ridden:** a mount goes in its own slot, and while you ride, your boots are only boots. Gear for the road on foot does nothing in the saddle.

## Errands

The **Friddik brothers** send timed errands between each other, a letter here, a parcel there, and now and then something everyday you must bring yourself. Each leg pays about forty gold and seventy-five Wayfaring on top of the walk. A leg has a limit, worked out from the roads at a new walker's pace, and a late leg ends the errand there. The Quests & People page has the rest.

## Connections

- **Husbandry** raises the horses you ride.
- **Carpentry** builds the boats you sail.
- **Crafting** sews the cordage every boat is rigged with.
- **Every other trade** is waiting at the far end of a road, which is why this one is never wasted.
