I have been bitten, butted, clawed and once, memorably, sat on, by a great many things in Talaran. I should say at the outset that I was not good at hitting them back.

Melee is the trade of hitting them back. It is the one trade where the thing you are working on objects. A tree does not mind being felled. A rat minds a great deal, and says so with its teeth.

## Where and how to start

On Taiar, the gentlest fighting is at **The Docks** in Talador. The rats there are the weakest creatures on the island, which makes them the right size for you.

1. Go to Talador and down to The Docks.
2. Ask for a fight. A rat will come at you shortly; you see it coming.
3. Fight until you have had enough, then **flee**. Travelling anywhere ends a fight as well.

You need nothing to begin. With nothing in your hand you fight with your fists, which crush, and which frighten nobody. Buy or forge a proper weapon as soon as you can.

Before you go, read the [Combat](/manual/systems/combat) page. It covers health, food and what happens when you lose, which is the part people wish they had read first.

## Training path

Melee pays the same for every kill whatever you hold, so the level decides only what you may hold, and how hard and how surely you swing it. The weapon matters more than the level. A trained fighter with a poor blade loses to a novice with a good one, for a while.

## Holding a weapon

Every weapon is held one of three ways, and the choice is the whole of a fighter's style:

- **A pair** swings quickest and leaves no room for a shield.
- **One hand** is steady, and leaves the other hand free for a shield, which is worth more than it looks.
- **Both hands** is slow and heavy. Every blow counts; there are fewer of them.

None of the three is the best. They trade speed against weight against cover, and they come out close to even over an hour.

**Aim** decides whether a blow lands. **Power** decides how hard. Each weapon also carries one kind of damage, pierce, slash or crush, and creatures are not equally soft to all three. A crab laughs at a sword. Your Bestiary learns which is which as you go.

## Tools and gear

**Equipped:** the weapon, in your main hand. A shield goes in the other, if the weapon allows one.

**Carried:** food. That is all. Fighting is the lightest trade to pack for and the heaviest to lose, as the Combat page explains.

## Connections

A fighter is a smith's best customer and his most regular, because weapons wear out.

- **Smithing** forges every weapon on the list, from ore **Mining** brings up.
- **Carpentry** and **Crafting** supply the hafts and the leather wrapping.
- **Fishing** and **Cooking** keep you standing.
- **Defense** and **Constitution** climb beside Melee on every kill. Your combat level is the average of the three.

The fight pays all three at once, so you never need to choose which to train. You need only choose what to carry.¹

¹ I once carried three weapons into the Deep Wood to be ready for anything. You may hold one. I was ready for nothing, and very well armed for it.
