Melee is how hard and how accurately you hit in a fight. It rises with every kill, along with Defense and Constitution, and its level decides which weapons you can use.

## Where and how to start

On Taiar, the easiest fights are at **The Docks** in Talador, where the Dock Rats are the weakest creatures on the island. **Georold** there teaches you the basics.

1. Go to Talador and choose **The Docks**.
2. Choose **Fight**. A creature comes at you shortly.
3. Fight as long as you like, then **Flee**. Starting a journey also ends a fight.

You need nothing to start. Without a weapon you fight with your fists, which crush and do little damage. Get a weapon as soon as you can.

**Antura** is for fighters of level 13 and up. Its fighting places, in order: the **Burners' Yard** at Mettlefell, the **Haunted Shallows** at Farvick Narrows, **the Horns** at Vergehorns, and the **High Passes** of Yondereach, where fights are long. Some places let you choose your creature; the High Passes send whatever is there. The tables below list every place and its levels.

On the islets, a guarded cache is watched by a creature from the sea. These are single long fights that pay like several ordinary kills, and some of these creatures have special attacks. See [Sailing & the Islets](/manual/systems/sailing-and-the-islets).

Read [Combat](/manual/systems/combat) before your first fight. It covers health, food, and what happens if you die.

## Weapons

Every kill pays the same Melee experience whatever you hold. Your level decides which weapons you can use and how well you use them. The weapon matters more than the level.

## Weapon styles

Each weapon is held one of three ways:

- **A pair**: the fastest swings, and no shield.
- **One hand**: steady, with the other hand free for a shield.
- **Both hands**: slow, heavy blows.

Over an hour, the three come out close to even. They trade speed, weight, and protection.

**Aim** decides whether a blow lands. **Power** decides how hard it lands. Each weapon also does one damage type: **pierce**, **slash**, or **crush**. Creatures are weaker to some types than others; your [Bestiary](/manual/systems/bestiary) records which as you fight them.

## Changing weapons in a fight

You hold one weapon at a time, but can carry several. Weapons in your pack are listed beside the fight, with shields below them when you hold a one-handed weapon. Switch to one and the fight continues with the new weapon from that moment.

## Rings and charms

A few rings and charms, made with Crafting from creature parts, add aim or power wherever you fight: the **Knuckle Ring** or **Heartstone Ring** for power, the **Jackalope Charm** or **Kelpie's Bridle Charm** for aim. They use no light from your Talar reservoir.

## Rare weapons

Some creatures occasionally drop a weapon nobody forged: a horn, a bone, an old blade. Rare weapons are better than any forged weapon of their level and last a very long time. Only the pawnbroker buys them. The Bestiary shows which creatures drop them as you fight.

## Tools and gear

- **Equipped:** your weapon in your main hand, and a shield in your off hand if the weapon allows one.
- **Carried:** food. See [Combat](/manual/systems/combat).

Weapons wear out and break; carry a spare.

## Connections

- **Smithing** forges every weapon, from ore brought by **Mining**: Ambren for tier 1, **Serph** (Smithing 13) for tier 2. **Merrick** in Talador and **Wystan** in Mettlefell sell them.
- **Carpentry** and **Crafting** supply hafts and leather wrapping.
- **Fishing** and **Cooking** supply food.
- **Defense** and **Constitution** rise with Melee on every kill. Your combat level is the average of the three.
