Farming grows crops in fields on your homestead. You till, sow, and come back later to harvest. Crops grow in real time, whether you are online or not.

## Where and how to start

You need a **farmstead** before you can have fields. On Taiar, go to **Novita** and talk to **Georgic**, who walks you through it.

Raising a farmstead costs **500 planks, 500 granite blocks, and 1,000 nails**, and takes about ten minutes of work. You need a **mallet** in your main hand and a **saw** in your off hand. Building pays **Carpentry** experience, not Farming.

On **Antura**, the homestead ground is at **Farefield**. Every island has its own homestead, raised the same way at the same cost, with its own fields, pens, and storage. A homestead on one island does nothing on another.

## The steps

1. **Enclose** a field with fencing and a stone border. Three minutes.
2. **Till** the soil. Ninety seconds, with a hoe equipped.
3. **Sow** seeds, a few seconds each, ten to a full field.
4. **Wait** while the crop grows.
5. **Harvest**, a little slower than sowing. This is the step that pays you.

## Tools and gear

- **Hoe**, equipped in your main hand, to till. A Serph hoe is the right one for Antura; an Ambren one works, slower.
- **Mallet** in your main hand and **saw** in your off hand, to raise a farmstead or fence a field.
- **Bucket**, carried in your pack, to tend.

## Soil

Soil is **depleted**, **ordinary**, or **rich**. It changes **how much you harvest**, and nothing else. Experience is the same on any soil.

- Rich soil gives about a quarter more than ordinary; depleted gives nearly half less.
- Hungry crops lower the soil a step when harvested. Legumes (peas, broad beans) raise it.
- A field left empty recovers about a step every eighteen hours.
- Manure raises it at once.
- Pulling up a strawberry bed, raspberry row, or gooseberry bush lowers the soil a step.

## Experience from each field

Your first field pays full experience. Each further field pays a little less, though never nothing. The farm screen shows each field's share. The work and the harvest are the same on every field; only the experience share changes.

## Crops belong to their island

**Taiar's crops grow only in Taiar's soil, and Antura's only in Antura's.**

Antura's six crops start at **Farming 13**: **Barley**, **Cabbage**, **Leek**, **Beet**, **Broad Beans**, and **Gooseberry**. They take a day or more each to grow and pay more than Taiar's. Broad Beans improve the soil, as peas do. Gooseberry is a bush: like strawberries, it stays in the ground and fruits again.

No merchant sells Antura's seeds. The **Seed Ground** at Farefield gives all six to foragers of level 13 and up, and the strandline on Antura's islets gives a few more.

**Barley** comes out as barley, with no threshing. Mill it at your homestead: three barley make two flour.

## More than one homestead

Antura's homestead holds at most **ten fields**; Taiar's holds twenty.

The experience share counts **every field you own on every island together**, highest-tier island first: Antura's fields take the top places, then Taiar's. A second homestead does not reset the share. The farm screen tells you when another island's fields rank ahead of the ones you are looking at.

## Tending

Tending a field with a bucket takes a tenth off its remaining growing time. It takes fifteen seconds a field.

## Connections

- **Carpentry** builds the farmstead and every fence.
- **Mining** supplies the granite for field borders.
- **Cooking** uses your grain and vegetables.
- **Husbandry** shares your homestead. Barley, beans, beet, and cabbage are animal feed as well as food.
- **Foraging** finds the seeds for Antura's fields.

Raise Carpentry before Farming, or buy the planks and nails from someone who has.
