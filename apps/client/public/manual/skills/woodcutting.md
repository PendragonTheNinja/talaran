Woodcutting looks like Mining with leaves on. Same rhythm, same rules about tools, the same business of going to a thing and hitting it until it gives in.

I believed that for a year and a half. I was wrong, and the reason is two sections down.

## Where and how to start

Find a tree. Equip an axe. Swing.

Every stand of timber states the level it wants and the grade of axe it would prefer. Work within your means at first. An axe beneath the job still cuts. It just cuts slowly, on every swing, for as long as you insist on using it.

## Tools and gear

The arithmetic is Mining's exactly, and worth knowing in both trades:

- Every level you stand above what a tree requires trims half a percent off the work, up to half at best.
- An axe **above** the required grade speeds you further, to a ceiling of about a third at three grades over.
- An axe **below** grade adds roughly forty percent per grade short.

Those are lopsided. A better axe than you need is a small luxury. A worse one is a tax on every swing.

## What your skill does to the timber

Timber comes out poor, fine, or excellent.

**Your skill changes which.**

Every level you stand above the tree's requirement tilts the odds: poor gets rarer, excellent gets more common. The tilt has a limit. Poor never vanishes entirely, and excellent never quite takes over.

Stone does not behave this way. An outcrop decided what it was long before you arrived, and the finest miner in Talaran pulls the same proportions from it as the worst. A tree gives better timber to a better hand on the axe.

So going back to humble trees is less of a waste than it looks. Return to the wood you first learned on when you are forty levels past it, and you will find it has become a much better wood.

## Connections

- **Carpentry** is where every log goes. There is honest money in selling logs, and a good deal more in working your own timber.
- **Hunting** needs arrow shafts, which need timber, which needs you.
- **Farming** needs fence panels, which need planks, which need logs.
- **Mining** is Woodcutting's twin. Learn either and you have most of the other for nothing.

If you take up only one gathering trade, take Woodcutting. More things here are made of wood than of anything else, and the timber gets better as you do.
