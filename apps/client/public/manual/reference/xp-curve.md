Every trade in Talaran climbs the same ladder.

I say so before the numbers arrive, because it is the most useful fact in this appendix and easy to lose among the figures. Mining, Cooking, Wayfaring and Hunting do not each have a ladder of their own. There is one, and it is the same on every island this world will ever have. The trades differ only in how quickly you earn the rungs.

So a level in a trade you find tedious costs exactly what a level in your favorite costs. Talaran plays no favorites and holds no grudges.

## What the shape of it means

Look at the early rungs, then at the late ones, and you will see the bargain on offer.

The first ten levels of anything arrive almost as a courtesy. You will pass through them wondering what the fuss was about. This is deliberate: every trade is trying to show you what it is before it asks you for anything.

Then it steepens, and keeps steepening, and by the upper reaches a single level is a campaign in itself.

I have opinions about this, but the useful observation is a practical one. **The cheapest levels in Talaran are always the ones you have not started yet.** A man at level ninety in one trade and level one in nine others is sitting on an enormous quantity of very cheap progress, and often does not realize it.

None of that argues against mastery. It argues for knowing what mastery costs before you set out after it.
