Every trade in Talaran climbs the same ladder.

I want to state that plainly before the numbers arrive, because it is the most useful fact in this appendix and it is easy to lose among the figures. Mining and Cooking and Equitation and Hunting do not have separate ladders of differing steepness. There is one ladder, and it is the same on every island this world will ever have. What differs between the trades is only how quickly you earn the rungs.

Which means a level in a trade you find tedious costs exactly what a level in your favourite costs. Talaran plays no favourites and holds no grudges.

## What the shape of it means

Look at the early rungs and then at the late ones, and you will see the honest bargain being offered.

The first ten levels of anything arrive almost as a courtesy. You will pass through them wondering what the fuss was about. This is deliberate: every trade is trying to show you what it is before it asks you for anything.

Then it steepens, and keeps steepening, and by the upper reaches a single level is a genuine campaign.

I have opinions about this, but the useful observation is a practical one. **The cheapest levels in Talaran are always the ones you have not started yet.** A man at level ninety in one trade and level one in nine others is sitting on an enormous quantity of very cheap progress, and frequently does not realise it.

None of that argues against mastery. It argues for knowing what mastery costs before you set out after it.
