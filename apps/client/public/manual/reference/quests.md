Every quest in the game, and who gives it. Quests you have not been offered do not appear in your own Quests menu until someone offers them to you.

{{data:quests}}

## Columns

- **Teaches**: the trade the quest is built around. A tutorial quest is usually the fastest way into a new trade.
- **Ask**: the person who gives it, and where they are.
- **Steps**: how many things the quest asks for. Work you have already done usually counts as soon as you accept.

Your own progress is in your Quests menu, not here.
