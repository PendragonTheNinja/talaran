I am going to break a small rule on this page.

Everywhere else in this manual I talk about Talaran as though it were simply the world. That is a polite fiction, and I am fond of it. But you are looking at the world through a frame, and it would be unkind to pretend you are not. So, the frame. Briefly, and then we need never mention it again.

## The four keys worth knowing

Most of what you will look up from day to day sits behind four keys.

- **F1** for your Skills
- **F2** for your Stats
- **F3** for your Quests
- **F4** for your Feats

Learn those and you know where most of the furniture is. I held out against them for far too long, on the grounds that a scholar ought to find things by hand.

## What sits where

Your **inventory** sits down the left, along with the paper doll of what you are wearing. Glance at the doll now and then. It shows what you are actually wearing and holding, which is not always what you own.

The **middle** is wherever you are, and whatever you may do there. Work appears when you arrive somewhere it can be done, and not before. If a trade seems to be missing, you are probably standing in the wrong place. I have made that diagnosis of myself more often than I care to record.

**Chat** runs along the bottom, in several channels, because Talaran is talkative and not all of it is talking to you:

- **World** reaches everyone, everywhere, across every island. Loud, occasionally useful.
- **Region** reaches the island you are standing on. More likely to concern you.
- **Guild** reaches your own people, if you have found any.
- **Trade** is for buying and selling. Skip it while you are new, and come back when you have something worth haggling over.
- **Help** is for questions and the people who answer them. Nobody will think less of you for asking. People rather like being asked.

Mind the difference between the first two, because it will matter more as Talaran grows. World is everyone. Region is only the island under your feet.

## The panels along the top

The row across the top opens the larger rooms: your messages, the forum, your guild, the highscores, the news and this manual. They open over the world rather than taking you away from it, and opening one puts the last away.

Two more of them are worth knowing early. The **Journal** is a notebook of your own, kept with your character, for whatever you would otherwise forget. **Events** shows what is happening around the island today, where, and how long it has left to run.

## And that is the frame

Good. The fiction resumes on the next page, and I intend to keep it up for the remainder of the manual.
