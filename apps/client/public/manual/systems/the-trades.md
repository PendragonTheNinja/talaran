No trade in Talaran supplies itself completely. Every trade, followed far enough, needs something from another.

## An example: one arrow

- The **head** is metal: Smithing made it, from ore that Mining dug.
- The **shaft** is wood: Carpentry cut it, from a log Woodcutting felled, at a bench that Carpentry built.
- The **feathers** come from Hunting, Husbandry, or a creature drop.

The hunter who shoots it then supplies hides to Crafting, meat to Cooking, and young animals to Husbandry. Husbandry's cattle give leather, and Crafting turns leather back into gear.

## What this means in practice

- **Train several trades early.** A player with five trades at level 20 can supply themselves; a player with one trade at level 90 has to buy everything else.
- **A shortage is often in another trade.** A hunter short of arrows needs Smithing or Woodcutting, not more Hunting.
- **Gathering trades have more buyers than they seem to.** Granite goes to smiths' customers, carpenters, farmers, and anyone building a workstation.
- **Train the trade that supplies the one you want.** Carpentry before Hunting or Farming; Mining before Smithing.

## What feeds what

- **Woodcutting** → Carpentry.
- **Mining** → Smithing, Carpentry, Farming, and Crafting.
- **Carpentry** → Farming, Crafting, Hunting, and its own workstations.
- **Smithing** → Hunting, Carpentry, Farming, and every trade that uses a tool.
- **Hunting** → Crafting, Cooking, and Husbandry.
- **Husbandry** → Crafting and Cooking.
- **Farming** → Cooking and Husbandry.
- **Foraging** → Cooking, Farming, and Crafting.
- **Crafting** takes from almost every trade and makes gear.
- **Cooking** comes at the end of most chains.

## Guilds

A full chain might span five trades. You can train all five, or join a [guild](/manual/systems/guilds) and share the work.
