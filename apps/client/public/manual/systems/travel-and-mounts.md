How travel works: walking, riding, the time limits on journeys, travel gear, and crossing between islands. Everything here applies on every island; only distances and places differ. Taiar is about a hundred kilometers from end to end.

## Walking and riding

Both pay **Wayfaring**.

- **On foot**, each Wayfaring level makes your journeys a little shorter. Early levels help most; later ones keep helping, by less.
- **Mounted**, the mount sets the pace. Each mount has a starting pace and a best pace, and your Wayfaring level moves you from one toward the other. The Rouncey, for example:

{{data:item-stats:Rouncey}}

Walking and riding bonuses do not combine. While riding, walking gear does nothing.

## The limit

**No journey can take less than a tenth of its base time**, whatever your mount, level, or gear.

## Walking gear

A staff in your hands and boots on your feet take a little off every journey on foot. They add together, and add on top of Wayfaring. Gear only works when worn.

## Experience

**You are paid for the journey's base time, not the time it actually took.** Getting faster never lowers your experience.

Riding pays less than walking for the same road. Only walkers find things at the roadside.

## Crossing the water

There are no roads between islands. Two ways across:

- **The ferry** between Talador and Wendhaven: 50 gold, eight minutes, open to anyone. It pays no experience.
- **Your own boat:** a Skiff or Cog makes the same crossing and pays Wayfaring at about the riding rate. A boat has a starting pace and a best pace, and Wayfaring closes the gap. A Cog at Wayfaring 13 crosses in about five minutes.

Nothing is found at sea on a crossing, and walking gear does nothing on the water. The **sailor's set** helps instead: each piece worn takes 1% off every sea journey. See [Wayfaring](/manual/skills/wayfaring) and [Sailing & the Islets](/manual/systems/sailing-and-the-islets).

## Antura's roads

Antura's roads run a little longer on average than Taiar's. Everything above applies the same. Walkers on Antura's roads can find sea thrift, spring gentian, meadowsweet for brewing, feathers along the shore, and rarer things.

## Walk or ride?

- **Ride** when you need to get somewhere quickly.
- **Walk** when you have time: more experience per road, and roadside finds.

Travel takes real time. You can chat, manage your pack, or read the Manual while you go.
