There comes a moment in every trade when you have more things than you can carry. Talaran's answer to that moment is property.

## Property is a place you own

A homestead is not a workstation, and the difference matters.

A **workstation** is a tool that has become a place: the smith's anvil, the tanner's rack. It does one job.

A **homestead** is a place that holds other things. It does no job by itself. It is ground with your name on it, and what happens on that ground is up to you.

On Taiar, the farmstead at **Novita** is the homestead you will meet first. It is not the only shape property will take, and Taiar will not be the only island where you may hold some.

## What it costs

Building a homestead is a serious piece of Carpentry. The farmstead's particular bill is on the [Farming](/manual/skills/farming) page.

The pattern is worth knowing even if farming does not interest you. Property is **built**, not bought. You pay for it in timber, stone and nails, not coin, so the road to owning land runs through the gathering trades whether you like it or not.

I like this about Talaran more than almost anything else. You cannot arrive wealthy and skip to the end. Somebody had to cut the trees, and on your homestead that somebody was you.

## Storage, and the thing everybody misunderstands

Your homestead stores things. Fifty slots of them.

The arithmetic is better than it sounds. **One slot holds one kind of item, in any quantity.**

Fifty *kinds*, then, and not fifty items. Four thousand planks take one slot. A single trophy also takes one slot, and I would gently suggest it has not earned it.

So storage is kind to the hoarder of a few things and hard on the collector of many. Store bulk materials and you will never come close to filling it. Store one of everything you have ever seen and you will fill it in a fortnight, and then write to me about it.

## Why you want one earlier than you think

New arrivals treat property as a reward for later and leave it far too long. It starts paying the day it exists.

Once you have somewhere to put four thousand planks, a gathering trip is no longer limited by what you can carry. It is limited by how long you feel like going. That is a much better way to work.

Build early. Build badly if you must. The planks will still be there.

## Connections

- **Carpentry** builds it, and is paid handsomely for doing so.
- **Mining** and **Woodcutting** supply the materials.
- **Farming** happens on it, and on Taiar is the reason most people build one at all.
