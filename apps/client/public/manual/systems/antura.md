Antura is the second island, across the strait from Talador. It is **tier 2**, for levels **13 to 24**. It has no tutors, no lessons, and no public benches: you bring your own tools.

## Getting there

- **The ferry** from **Talador** to **Wendhaven**: 50 gold, eight minutes, open to anyone. It pays no Wayfaring.
- **Your own boat** makes the crossing faster and pays Wayfaring. A Skiff can do it; a Cog takes about five minutes.

At a port, the ferry, the slip, and your boats are all under the **Sail** button.

## The places

- **Wendhaven** (port): the ferry and the slip; **Antura's shop ground**; a **workbench** with room for **your own lapidary** and a **Crafting Bench** for sewing and camp work; the **Antura Museum**; the **Gaster's Gaze**, a taproom kept by **Odda Fenn**; **Maude** the craftswoman, **Gil** the provisioner (buys only), and **Osric** the pawnbroker.
- **Farefield** (farmland): **Antura's homestead ground** (ten fields at most); room for **your own hearth**; and the **Seed Ground**, for foragers of level 13 and up.
- **Mettlefell** (forge town): room for **your own smithy**, forge and kiln; **Wystan** the smith, who sells every Serph tool, weapon, and piece of armor; **Ashby** the carpenter; **the cask**, the only place in Talaran to brew Ale; and the **Burners' Yard**, the island's easiest fighting.
- **Underway** (mine): **granite** from Mining 13, with garnet, and **Serph** veins.
- **Bidewater** (lake): tench, rudd, zander, and the Glassback, which only bites at night.
- **Farvick Narrows** (strait): mackerel, pollock, sea bass, and turbot; the **Tidal Shore**, for samphire and salt, from Foraging 19; and the **Haunted Shallows**, for fighting.
- **Yondereach** (high passes): the **High Passes**, Antura's hardest fighting, for level 22 and up.
- **Vergehorns** (twin headlands): **the Horns**, for fighters of level 19 to 21.

## What Antura has

Its own homestead, shop, and tally boards (built with Serph nails); its own museum; crops that grow only there; its own fish, creatures, and road finds; and eight islets, which need a Cog.

## What Antura does not have

- **No Rift and no church.** Gems are filled and spells learned on Taiar.
- **No public benches.** You can set up three benches of your own: a **smithy in Mettlefell**, a **lapidary and Crafting Bench in Wendhaven**, and a **hearth at Farefield**.
- **No carpentry bench, tannery, spinning wheel, or loom.** Timber work and tanning are done on Taiar.
- **No new timber or kiln wood.** Lanai planks and Charc come over from Taiar.

So much of Antura's work goes back and forth: mine, smith, cut garnets, cook, and fight on Antura; carry the rest to Taiar to work; bring what you made back. A Cog and a good Wayfaring level help.

## Owning things on both islands

You can have **one homestead and one shop on each island**. Each is separate, with its own storage, managed where it stands. Your fields and pens count together across both islands for experience, Antura's first. See [The Homestead](/manual/systems/the-homestead) and [Farming](/manual/skills/farming).

## Where to start

Arriving at level 13, go to Underway with a pickaxe, or to Farefield's Seed Ground with a foraging knife. Both open at 13.
