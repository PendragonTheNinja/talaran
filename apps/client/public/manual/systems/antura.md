Taiar is where everybody begins. Antura is where you go once you have stopped beginning.

It lies across the strait from Talador, bigger and wilder, with longer roads and colder water. It is **tier 2**, for levels **13 to 24**, and it does not pretend otherwise. There are no tutors here, no lessons, and no public bench lending you its tools. Antura assumes you have made every tool you need at least once already and would like to get on. Its theme, if a place can have a theme, is setting out.

## Getting there

- **The ferry** runs from **Talador** to **Wendhaven**: fifty gold, eight minutes, open to anyone, and it pays no Wayfaring.
- **Your own boat** makes the same crossing faster and pays Wayfaring at sea's rate. A Skiff will do it; a Cog does it in about five minutes.

At a port, the ferry, the slip, and your own boats are all under one **Sail** in the place's links.

## The places

- **Wendhaven**, the port. The ferry and the slip; **Antura's shop ground**; a **workbench**, with room for **your own lapidary** and a **Crafting Bench** for sewing and camp work; the **Antura Museum**; the **Gaster's Gaze**, a taproom kept by **Odda Fenn**; and **Maude** the craftswoman, **Gil** the provisioner, who only buys, and **Osric** the pawnbroker. Nan keeps a window box here, and the harbormaster's clerk keeps a great many papers.
- **Farefield**, the farmland. **Antura's homestead ground**, ten fields at most; room for **your own hearth**; the **Seed Ground**, for foragers of 13; and two neighbors with a great deal to say about cabbages.
- **Mettlefell**, the forge town. Room for **your own smithy**, forge and kiln. **Wystan** the smith sells every Serph tool, weapon, and piece of armor; **Ashby** the carpenter sells his own work. The cookhouse keeps **the cask**, the only place in Talaran where Ale is brewed. The **Burners' Yard** is the gentlest fighting on the island.
- **Underway**, the mine. **Granite** from Mining 13, with garnet in it, and **Serph** veins.
- **Bidewater**, the lake. Tench, rudd, zander, and the Glassback, which comes up only at night.
- **Farvick Narrows**, the strait. Mackerel, pollock, sea bass, and turbot; the **Tidal Shore**, for samphire and salt, from Foraging 19; and the **Haunted Shallows**, where the things in the water are not fish.
- **Yondereach**, the high passes. The **High Passes** are Antura's hardest fighting, for 22 and up.
- **Vergehorns**, twin headlands. **The Horns**, for fighters of 19 to 21, and a very great many birds.

## What Antura has, and what it does not

**It has** its own homestead, shop, and tally boards, each built of its own island's nails; its own museum; its own crops, which grow nowhere else; its own fish, foes, and finds on the road; and eight islets off its coast, which want a Cog.

**It does not have** a Rift or a church, so every stone is still filled and every spell still learned on Taiar. It has no public benches, nobody to lend you tools, and three benches of your own: a **smithy in Mettlefell**, a **lapidary and Crafting Bench in Wendhaven**, and a **hearth at Farefield**, so Serph, garnet, Antura's scales and pelts, and its food can be worked where they are found. Every carpentry bench, tannery, wheel, and loom is still on Taiar, so Antura's timber work and hides go home to be worked. And it grows no timber or kiln wood Taiar does not, so Lanai planks and Charc cross the strait the other way.

Much of Antura's work therefore runs in a loop. Mine and smith there, cut your garnets, cook, and fight there; take the rest home to Taiar to make; sail back with what you made. A Cog and a decent Wayfaring are worth having for that alone.

## Owning things on two islands

You may keep **one homestead and one shop on each island**. They are separate places, each with its own store, managed where it stands. Your fields and pens are counted together across both for what they teach, Antura's first. The Homestead page and the Farming page have the details.

## Where to start

If you have just stepped off the ferry at 13 and do not know where to look first: walk to Underway with a pickaxe, or to Farefield with a forager's knife. Both open at 13, both feed something you will want later, and both are on the way to everything else.
