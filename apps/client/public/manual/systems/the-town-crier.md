Every so often the Maker wants to know what you think. He could ask in the forum, and some of you would read it. Instead he sends Hollin Bray.

Hollin is the Town Crier. He has a bell, a voice that carries over a market at noon, and no sense at all of when he is not wanted. When there is a question going round, he finds you, wherever you happen to be standing.

## How a question reaches you

The moment a question is put, or the next time you come back to Talaran if you were away, a notice is pinned over the scene in front of you. You need not go looking. That is rather the point.

You can answer it there and then. Most questions are a single tap, and the longer ones take you through a page at a time, with a **Back** for second thoughts.

Or you can say **Not now**. The notice comes down, and Hollin stays. You will find him among the people standing about you, listed with the others, and he follows you from place to place until you have answered or the question has closed. Tap his name and the notice goes back up. It is the same on every screen you play on: put it away on your phone and it stays away at your desk.

## What you may be asked

Most of it is ordinary: pick one, pick a few, give it a mark out of five. Some questions ask you to put things in order, or to share out a handful of points between them so the Maker can see how much you care, as well as what. Some show you items to choose between. A few just ask you to write.

Where a question allows it, there is a **Something else** answer for when none of the choices fits. Use it. The written answers are read, every one.

## The Crier's Chits

Answering pays you in **Crier's Chits**, a little stamped token for your trouble. Each question says how many it pays before you start. They are paid once a question, and only for an answer, so there is nothing to be gained by asking Hollin to repeat himself.

What chits buy is still being settled. Keep them. You will not regret it.

## Who is asked

Townsfolk with a registered name, not guests. A question also waits until you have been in Talaran a few days and put a few hours behind you, so that a crowd of new faces made for the purpose cannot carry a vote. The exact length is set on each question.

## When it closes

A question stays open for a set time and then closes, and Hollin moves on. If you answered, the next time you look in you will be shown how everyone else answered: the share each choice took, the average marks, the order things came out in. What people wrote stays between them and the Maker.

Your own answer is kept with your levels and time played as they were when you gave it. That is how the Maker tells what those just off the boat think from what the old hands do, which are often two quite different opinions.
