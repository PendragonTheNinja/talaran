The Town Crier, **Hollin Bray**, brings polls from the game's developer. Answering pays Crier's Chits.

## How a poll reaches you

When a poll opens, or the next time you log in if you were away, a notice appears over the scene. You can answer it there. Most polls take a single tap; longer ones go a page at a time, with a **Back** button.

Or choose **Not now**. The notice closes, and Hollin Bray appears in **Players Here** wherever you go until you answer or the poll closes. Tap his name to reopen it. Choosing Not now applies on every device you play on.

## Kinds of questions

Pick one; pick several; rate out of five; put things in order; share out points between options; choose between items; or write an answer. Where allowed, a **Something else** option lets you write your own answer. Written answers are read.

## Crier's Chits

Answering pays **Crier's Chits**. Each poll shows how many before you start. You are paid once per poll. What chits buy has not been decided yet.

## Who can answer

Registered players only, not guests. You also need to have played for a few days and a few hours; the exact amounts are set per poll.

## When a poll closes

Each poll is open for a set time. After it closes, players who answered are shown the results once: the share each choice got, average ratings, and the final order. Written answers are not shown to other players.

Your answer is stored with your levels and time played at the moment you answered, so the results can be compared between new and experienced players.
